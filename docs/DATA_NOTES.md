## Source and processing
- The embedded bitmaps on SI p.6 (S4, 501×733 px) and p.5 (S3, 529×718 px) were extracted losslessly with `pdfimages -j`. Both are **JPEG quality 75, 4:2:0 chroma subsampling** (inferred from the quantization tables).
- Sub-frames were segmented automatically: S4 has 29 frames, each cropped to 82×85 px; S3 has 31 frames, each 59×114 px. The times follow the labels printed in the SI. S3 has two frames labelled 1230 ns; both are kept (the second is `_1`).
- The jet colourmap was inverted by nearest-neighbour lookup against the 256-entry matplotlib jet table. After 8-bit rounding, entries 28–32 are the same pure blue, so tied entries map to their mean (otherwise there is a −2 gray bias right at threshold 27).

## Extraction error (closed-loop test, synthetic frames → jet → JPEG q75 → inversion)
- Gray RMS error ≈ 2 (requirement < 8). Total signal L within ±1–4% per frame; decay time τ_L within < 5%. Centroid error < 1 px.
- **Secondary metric (threshold 27) has a systematic error.** jet is almost flat around gray 22–32, so the threshold is poorly resolved after JPEG. The threshold radius is about **−1 px (≈ −10 µm)** low, and **unreliable** in faint frames with peak < 60 (S4 ≥ 580 ns; S3 ≥ 825 ns). This affects only the S7-style descriptive curves, not the main judgement.

## Background and saturation
- Background measured in the frame corners: 2–4 gray, noise ≈ 1.5–3. (v1 inferred about 20 from S7a; that was wrong. It is the peak of frames below threshold.)
- Saturated (≥250) pixels are present: high fluence 50–160 ns; low fluence 10–340 ns. **L in these frames is only a lower bound.** The low-fluence 340 ns frame lies inside the P1 window 300–630 ns and is slightly underestimated (103 saturated pixels).
