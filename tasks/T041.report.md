The Cu fraction uses trapezoidal time integration of P_las times f_Cu divided by the integral of P_las over the saved window. The half-peak window is a descriptive choice for this report. Each run uses its own peak gas absorption. During that window 90% of the absorbed power stayed within 3.5 to 6.25 um of the face across the five runs. The largest individual 90% heights occurred much later, at 201 to 247 um, when absorbed power had fallen below 0.005% of the corresponding peak. Thus the snapshots show a high absorption region in the weak tail while the main energy deposition remains near the face.

At about 50 ns the waveform and Gaussian v1rs runs both have a 90% absorption height of 3.5 um. The waveform hot region reaches 135.09 um compared with 83.50 um for Gaussian v1rs. These heights use the highest cell above 10 kK anywhere in the mesh. An emission centroid requires an emission-weighted image measurement.

The CSV names under `results/T041/` encode the batch, member and iteration. Both G0 and G1 grid comparisons use their stated iteration. All times are the native solver snapshot times. The diagnostic read existing fields and completed without changes to the solver or its inputs.
